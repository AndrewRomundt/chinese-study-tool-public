# dialogue | 经理的安排 | The manager's plan
经理: 李文，去北京的航班推迟了。 | Li Wen, the flight to Beijing has been postponed.

经理: 推迟到明天上午。所以我们得{děi}这样安排： | Until tomorrow morning. So here's how we'll arrange things:

李文: 金融公司的领导也要见您。 | The head of the finance company also wants to meet you.
经理: 那就安排在后天上午吧。 | Then arrange it for the morning of the day after tomorrow.
李文: 好的，我马上打一通电话。 | Fine, I'll make a call right away.

# dialogue | 您贵姓？ | What's your surname?
王小明: 您好！ | Hello!
李文: 你好！ | Hi!
王小明: 您贵姓？ | What's your surname?
李文: 我姓李，叫李文。你呢？你叫什么名字？ | My surname is Li, and my name is Li Wen. And you? What's your name?
王小明: 我叫王小明。 | I'm Wang Xiaoming.
李文: 你是中国人吗？ | Are you Chinese?
王小明: 是，我是中国人。你也是中国人吗？ | Yes, I'm Chinese. Are you Chinese too?
李文: 是，我也是中国人。 | Yes, I'm Chinese too.

@ 1.2
# dialogue | 你说汉语吗？ | Do you speak Chinese?
马克: 你好！你是老师吗？ | Hi! Are you the teacher?
王小明: 不是，我们是同学！ | No, we're classmates!
马克: 你好，同学！我叫马克。你叫什么名字？ | Hello, classmate! I'm Mark. What's your name?
王小明: 我叫王小明。你是哪国人？ | I'm Wang Xiaoming. Where are you from?
马克: 我是美国人。你呢？ | I'm American. And you?
王小明: 我是中国人。你说汉语吗？ | I'm Chinese. Do you speak Chinese?
马克: 我说英语，也说汉语。你说英语还是法语？ | I speak English, and Chinese too. Do you speak English or French?
王小明: 我只说汉语。 | I only speak Chinese.
马克: 好，我们都说汉语！ | Fine, we'll both speak Chinese!
? 王小明是老师吗？ | Is Wang Xiaoming the teacher? | No, he's a classmate; Yes, he's the teacher; It doesn't say
? 马克是哪国人？ | Where is Mark from? | America; Canada; Australia
? 王小明说英语吗？ | Does Wang Xiaoming speak English? | No, only Chinese; Yes; He speaks French
? 他们都说什么？ | What language do they both speak? | Chinese; English; French
